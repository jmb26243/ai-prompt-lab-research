AI & PROGRAMMING RESEARCH PROJECT

Experiment with prompts. Discover what works.

AI Prompt Lab explores how different prompting techniques influence AI-generated responses. The goal is to create a space where students can experiment, evaluate results, and learn from their experiences.

The Big Question

How can a simple application help students experiment with AI prompts, evaluate the results, and learn which prompting approaches work better?

RESEARCH EXPERIMENTATION LEARNING

THE PROJECT

What is AI Prompt Lab?

A proposed learning tool designed to make AI experimentation more organized and meaningful.

⌨

Experiment

Create different prompts and record the AI-generated responses.

◎

Evaluate

Rate responses, write observations, and identify what worked well.

↗

Improve

Review previous experiments and use what you learned to improve future prompts.

WHO IS IT FOR?

Designed for curious learners.

The intended audience is college students and beginners who want to better understand generative AI and how the way we write prompts can influence the responses we receive.

Our intended users

  • College students exploring AI tools
  • Beginners learning prompt engineering
  • Students comparing different AI responses
  • Curious users who want to learn through experimentation
