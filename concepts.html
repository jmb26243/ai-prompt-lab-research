EXPLORING THE POSSIBILITIES

Creative Project Concepts

Comparing two programming ideas and choosing a project that balances usefulness, creativity, and technical feasibility.

THE IDEATION PROCESS

Two ideas. One direction.

Before selecting a project, we considered two different applications designed to help students organize and improve their learning experiences.

CONCEPT A SELECTED
✦
AI-powered experimentation

AI Prompt Lab

Experiment with prompts. Discover what works.

A learning tool that allows students to record AI prompts, save generated responses, evaluate results, and revisit previous experiments.

Core features

  • Personal user accounts
  • Prompt and response recording
  • Ratings and personal notes
  • Private experiment history
  • Account settings and maintenance
Intended audience: College students and beginners learning to use generative AI.
CONCEPT B ALTERNATIVE
◷
Organize your learning routine

Study Session Tracker

Organize study time. Track your progress.

A productivity application that allows students to record study sessions, track time spent on different subjects, and review their study habits.

Core features

  • Personal user accounts
  • Study session recording
  • Subject and duration tracking
  • Private study history
  • Account settings and maintenance
Intended audience: College students who want to organize their study routines and monitor their time.

SIDE-BY-SIDE COMPARISON

Evaluating the concepts

Both ideas offer a useful student-focused workflow, but they differ in their purpose and technical focus.

Consideration AI Prompt Lab Study Session Tracker
Primary purpose Explore and evaluate AI prompts Organize study activities
Main interaction Create and evaluate prompt experiments Record study sessions
Saved records Prompts, responses, ratings, notes Subjects, duration, dates, notes
Dashboard Experiment history and ratings Study history and time summaries
AI connection Central to the project topic Not necessary
Technical challenges Text handling, evaluation, record ownership Time tracking, summaries, record ownership
Potential extension Comparing evaluation strategies Study goals and progress statistics

These are preliminary design estimates, not results from implemented prototypes or measured user tests.

TRADE-OFFS

What did we gain and give up?

Choosing one concept means prioritizing certain opportunities while postponing others.

✦

Why choose AI Prompt Lab?

  • Connects directly to AI and programming research.
  • Provides an opportunity to explore prompt evaluation.
  • Offers a meaningful setting for experimenting with design patterns.
  • Connects to interests in human-computer interaction and usability.
◷

What are we giving up?

  • A simpler time-tracking workflow.
  • Opportunities to explore study habits and productivity.
  • A potentially easier approach to measuring numerical progress.
  • A project focused more directly on student scheduling.
FINAL PROJECT SELECTION

AI Prompt Lab

AI Prompt Lab was selected because it offers a stronger connection to the research topic and creates an opportunity to explore how people interact with generative AI.

The project also provides a manageable way to practice full-stack development through user accounts, saved experiments, a private dashboard, and account management.
