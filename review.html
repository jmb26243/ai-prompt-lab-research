FEEDBACK & IMPROVEMENT

Peer Review

Gathering feedback, evaluating design decisions, and improving AI Prompt Lab through collaboration.

WHY PEER REVIEW?

A fresh perspective makes a difference.

Peer review gives another person an opportunity to explore the project, understand its decisions, and identify areas that may need clarification or improvement.

01. Explore

The reviewer will explore the research website and open at least one original research source.

02. Understand

The reviewer will explain a project decision and identify an assumption or confusing section.

03. Improve

Feedback will be evaluated and documented through GitHub issues and project updates.

WALKTHROUGH QUESTIONS

What will we ask the reviewer?

These questions will guide the discussion during the project walkthrough.

01. Research source

Can you open one of the linked research sources and explain what information it provides?

02. Design decision

Can you explain why AI Prompt Lab was selected instead of the Study Session Tracker?

03. Assumptions

Is there an assumption, explanation, or part of the website that is unclear or needs more evidence?

FROM FEEDBACK TO ACTION

How will feedback be handled?

Each suggestion will be considered and assigned an appropriate decision.

Implement

Make the suggested improvement when it clearly benefits the project.

Investigate

Research or test the suggestion before deciding whether to make a change.

Defer

Save the suggestion for a later development stage when it becomes more relevant.

Decline

Explain why a suggestion will not be adopted when it does not fit the project goals.

GitHub issue and change log

GitHub issues will document the review feedback, decisions, and any resulting changes.
